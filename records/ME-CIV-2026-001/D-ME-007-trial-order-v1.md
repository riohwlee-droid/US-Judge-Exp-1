# Superior Court Order Granting Summary Judgment and Final Judgment

**SIMULATED SUPERIOR COURT ORDER.** Maine Superior Court, Cumberland County, CV-2025-418. Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC v. Penobscot Power Systems, Inc. Entered June 3, 2026 by fictional Justice Adrian Webb.
The defendant moves for summary judgment pursuant to M.R. Civ. P. 56(c). After review of the parties' Rule 56(h) statements, declarations, agreement and briefs, the Court grants the motion.
UNDISPUTED FOR PURPOSES OF MOTION: A qualifying notice was received Nov. 14 at 09:55. Severe weather affected the region; Provider's first departure plan was cancelled after a road closure. Equipment reached the site Nov. 16 at 07:20. Parties disagree on the availability of later routes, personnel and particular equipment. Plaintiff claims spoilage and response costs; Defendant contests those claimed losses.
The Agreement §3.1's twelve-hour obligation is expressly 'subject to §3.2.' Section 3.2's first sentence excludes Provider responsibility for delay caused by severe weather, road closure, or conditions outside its reasonable control. This language unambiguously covers the interval in dispute, beginning in the midst of a severe storm.

The second sentence of §3.2 instructs Provider to use 'commercially reasonable efforts to minimize' delays. The Court construes it as an operational aspiration within an express excuse, rather than an independent source of damages liability for a severe-weather delay. Otherwise the excuse would have no practical function. Read as a whole, the Agreement leaves losses from such weather delays with Customer.
Because undisputed severe weather caused the initial disruption and transport delay, the Court regards disagreement over alternative routes and backup equipment as immaterial. On this reading it need not determine whether any later failure to act was commercially reasonable. Plaintiff's damage and causation proof would independently present substantial questions, but the Court does not reach those issues as grounds for judgment.
ORDER: Defendant's motion for summary judgment is GRANTED. Judgment shall enter for Penobscot Power Systems, Inc. on the entire complaint. All relief sought by Plaintiff is DENIED. There are no remaining counts or parties. Costs may be pursued as allowed by rule.
Dated and entered June 3, 2026. /s/ Adrian Webb, Justice, Superior Court (fictional). The parties were served notice of judgment that date.
