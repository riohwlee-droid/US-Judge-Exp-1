# Notice of Appeal and Transcript-Order Certification

**SIMULATED FILED NOTICE.** Superior Court CV-2025-418; appeal designated ME-LC-2026-147. Filed with Cumberland County Superior Court June 16, 2026.
NOTICE: Plaintiff Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC appeals to the Maine Supreme Judicial Court, sitting as the Law Court, from the final judgment entered June 3, 2026 granting summary judgment in favor of Penobscot Power Systems, Inc.
The issues are the interpretation of §3.2 of the written Agreement and whether the summary-judgment record permits judgment as a matter of law. Plaintiff reserves issues properly presented in its briefs and does not ask the Law Court to hear new evidence.
The notice was filed 13 calendar days after judgment entry; counsel invokes M.R. App. P. 2B(c)(1). No tolling motion is represented to have been filed.
TRANSCRIPT / AUDIO REQUEST: The April 2, 2026 summary-judgment submission was decided without oral proceedings. Counsel requests any existing transcribed proceedings responsive to the appeal but confirms none are known as of filing. Trial court record consists of the docket, pleadings, Rule 56 affidavits, motion papers, and written judgment.
Service on appellee counsel certified June 16, 2026. /s/ Caroline Ames, Esq., counsel for Appellant.
