# Sworn Declarations, Dispatch Log and Road Bulletin Exhibits

**SIMULATED TRIAL-COURT EXHIBITS.** The following declarations and attachments were lodged with the Rule 56 papers. They are evidence proffered by parties, not adjudicated facts.
DECLARATION OF LEILA FROST (operations manager for Harborlight), sworn Feb. 26, 2026 before fictional Maine notary.
¶1. I oversee facility operations and have personal knowledge of the alarms and calls described.
¶2. Our refrigeration system depended on utility power in November 2024; we requested mobile equipment rather than representing that a permanent generator was on premises.
¶3. The utility feed failed at 05:40 Nov. 14; I read the panel clock. We called the Provider line at 09:55 when the outage had exceeded four hours.
¶4. We paid the standby retainer through the relevant coverage period. I did not retain another standby generator provider.
¶5. At 17:05 on Nov. 14 I called for an update. The representative said, in substance, 'we will reevaluate tomorrow.' I immediately sent a written status request, attached below.
¶6. A temporary unit was connected and powered at 07:20 Nov. 16. Utility power returned Nov. 17 at 13:10, according to our site log.
¶7. Our internal temperature monitor exceeded the target at 23:30 Nov. 14. The inventories subsequently discarded had a book value of $91,400, but I cannot personally establish when each item became unmerchantable.
¶8. We spent approximately $12,000 on emergency removal and disposal; some expenses would have arisen even with earlier backup. Supporting invoices exist in our accounting system but only the attached summary was submitted with this motion.
Executed: /s/ Leila Frost.
