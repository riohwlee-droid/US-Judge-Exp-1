# Emergency Generation Support Agreement — May 17, 2024

**SIMULATED CONTRACT — EXHIBIT A, FILED IN SUPERIOR COURT.** Effective May 17, 2024. Parties: Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC ("Customer") and Penobscot Power Systems, Inc. ("Provider").
RECITALS. Customer stores seafood inventory in temperature-controlled buildings near Ellsworth, Maine. Provider maintains mobile generator units and transport contracts. Parties desire a standby response arrangement; neither party guarantees continuous utility electricity.
§1 Scope and retainer. Customer shall pay $45,600 per twelve-month coverage period in monthly installments of $3,800. Provider shall maintain a workable dispatch plan for temporary generation rated no less than 180 kW. Customer shall provide clear safe vehicle access and reasonable hookup information.
§2 Notice. Customer may issue a mobilization notice by recorded dispatch telephone line after a utility interruption has lasted more than four continuous hours. Provider shall create a dispatch-log entry recording receipt, location and expected transport plan. The parties will promptly share known hazards.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

§3 Performance and delay.
§3.1 Following a qualifying notice, Provider shall furnish temporary generation capable of supporting Customer's refrigeration system and complete hookup at the facility within twelve hours, subject to §3.2.
§3.2 Provider shall not be responsible for delay caused by severe weather, road closure, or other conditions beyond its reasonable control. **Provider shall nevertheless use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize any such delay, including consideration of available alternate equipment or transport routes when practicable.**
§3.3 Nothing in this Agreement requires Provider to transport equipment in violation of a governmental closure order or place personnel in unreasonable danger. When an obstruction ceases, Provider shall resume performance with commercially reasonable dispatch.
§3.4 Customer is responsible for safe site access, lawful fuel supply after hookup and the accuracy of facility power requirements.
§4 Records. Provider shall preserve telephone logs, dispatch logs and communications regarding declared emergency requests for not less than two years; Customer shall preserve information concerning the outage and inventory affected.
§5 Charges. Actual third-party fuel expense and specified extra-rigging charges require reasonable documentation; the standby retainer alone does not purchase or transfer title to equipment.

§6 Claims and liability. The parties may seek remedies for proven breach of this Agreement subject to applicable law. Neither party guarantees against losses solely resulting from the utility's grid failure, and either may contest whether an alleged breach caused a claimed loss. No contractual cap or liquidated-damages schedule applies to the claim at issue.
§7 Integration and changes. This writing and its attached service-site sheet are the full agreement; changes require a signed writing. Conduct undertaken during an emergency does not by itself amend the express response standard.
SERVICE-SITE SHEET (incorporated): Ellsworth facility known to the parties as Harborlight Pier Road Cold Storage; 180 kW temporary requirement; gate contact Leila Frost; dispatcher telephone on agreement cover; access gate is east of warehouse; no on-site permanent backup generator is provided by Provider under this contract.
SIGNED: /s/ Morgan Bell, authorized member for Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC; /s/ Owen Sutherland, president for Penobscot Power Systems, Inc.; executed May 17, 2024.
