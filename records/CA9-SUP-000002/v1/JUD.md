UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
CENTRAL DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — WESTERN DIVISION
Mira L. Sedgewick v. Harborline Support Services, LLC — SIM-CDCA-25-CV-081 (fictional)
SEPARATE JUDGMENT — AUGUST 24, 2026
Pursuant to the order granting Defendant's motion for summary judgment entered this date, judgment is entered in favor of Harborline Support Services, LLC and against Mira L. Sedgewick on the sole claim for overtime compensation under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Plaintiff shall take nothing on the claim. This judgment resolves all claims between the parties. The Clerk is directed to close the district case. Any application for taxable costs or attorney fees shall be made separately in accordance with applicable rules. No determination of a specific amount of costs is made by this judgment.
Entered August 24, 2026.
/s/ Isla R. Bram, Deputy Clerk (fictional), at the direction of the Court.
