UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — SAN FRANCISCO DIVISION
United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
ORDER CONTINUING TRIAL — 2026-01-12
Following a status conference at which Defendant appeared with counsel and the United States was represented, the Court grants the limited continuance to 2026-03-09. The native recording arrived in discovery on January 8 in a proprietary container; the parties require time to obtain a mutually readable export and to compare the exported audio against the original. Defense counsel has identified a discrepancy in the accompanying index, not a claim that the recording was altered. Counsel also needs to review the award attachments and interview the two custodians. The Court finds that the requested interval is necessary for effective preparation with due diligence. On this record, the Court finds that the ends of justice served by this continuance outweigh the best interests of the public and the defendant in a speedy trial within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 3161(h)(7)(A). The period from this order through the continued setting is excluded to the extent authorized by that provision. The Court does not rely on calendar congestion or treat the parties' agreement as a waiver of the statutory calculation. All remaining time shall be calculated by the Clerk and counsel. The defendant remains released under existing conditions. The parties shall appear on the continued date; further delay requires a separate showing and findings.
/s/ Miriam V. Keene, United States District Judge (fictional).
