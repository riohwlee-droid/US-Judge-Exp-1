UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — SAN FRANCISCO DIVISION
United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
ORDER CONTINUING TRIAL — 2026-03-09
Following a status conference at which Defendant appeared with counsel and the United States was represented, the Court grants the limited continuance to 2026-05-04. The defense has received the readable recording but needs the vendor's original receipt and transfer ledger. The vendor identified an archived record requiring a supervised retrieval, and the parties have arranged production and authentication. Counsel must examine the archive and interview the technician before deciding whether the proposed stipulation is appropriate. The Court finds that the interval is reasonably required for effective preparation, not a delay caused by a lack of diligent government preparation. On this record, the Court finds that the ends of justice served by this continuance outweigh the best interests of the public and the defendant in a speedy trial within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 3161(h)(7)(A). The period from this order through the continued setting is excluded to the extent authorized by that provision. The Court does not rely on calendar congestion or treat the parties' agreement as a waiver of the statutory calculation. All remaining time shall be calculated by the Clerk and counsel. The defendant remains released under existing conditions. The parties shall appear on the continued date; further delay requires a separate showing and findings.
/s/ Miriam V. Keene, United States District Judge (fictional).
