UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — SAN FRANCISCO DIVISION
United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
ORDER CONTINUING TRIAL — 2026-05-04
Following a status conference at which Defendant appeared with counsel and the United States was represented, the Court grants the limited continuance to 2026-07-13. The vendor records have now been produced. Both parties have identified a finite remaining set of trial preparations: stipulated exhibit copies, review of the recorded interview in context, witness preparation, proposed jury instructions and the treatment of the award-specific attachments. Defense counsel requests the July 13 date to permit meaningful preparation. The Court finds that the additional time is needed for effective assistance and the orderly presentation of the contested recording, having considered a shorter interval and the parties' readiness. On this record, the Court finds that the ends of justice served by this continuance outweigh the best interests of the public and the defendant in a speedy trial within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 3161(h)(7)(A). The period from this order through the continued setting is excluded to the extent authorized by that provision. The Court does not rely on calendar congestion or treat the parties' agreement as a waiver of the statutory calculation. All remaining time shall be calculated by the Clerk and counsel. The defendant remains released under existing conditions. The parties shall appear on the continued date; further delay requires a separate showing and findings.
/s/ Miriam V. Keene, United States District Judge (fictional).
