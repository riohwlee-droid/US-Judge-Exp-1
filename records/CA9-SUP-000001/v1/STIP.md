United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
JOINT STIPULATIONS — JUNE 26, 2026
The parties stipulate solely to the following matters for purposes of the trial: (1) the Department of Commerce is a department of the United States and the equipment award was administered by it; (2) the April 7 interview occurred in San Francisco, California; (3) the recording offered as Joint Exhibit 5 accurately captures the audible words, subject to the parties' right to explain context; (4) the six serial identifiers in the property schedule refer to the same items in the March worksheet, certification and vendor records; (5) the two instruments identified in the vendor record were temporarily in vendor custody during the period from March 11 to March 21, 2025; and (6) the government is not alleging that any item was stolen or permanently missing. These stipulations do not admit the charged answer's meaning, falsity, knowledge, willfulness or materiality.

JOINT PRETRIAL DIRECTIONS
The government intends to offer the award attachment, worksheet and certification, transfer records, monitoring correspondence and interview recording. The defense may rely on the same materials and call the property coordinator, operations director and defendant if the defendant elects to testify. The parties agree to identify exhibit numbers consistently and not display material to the jury before admission. The transcript may be used with the recording as an aid; the recording controls any actual discrepancy. Counsel reserve objections to testimony beyond a witness's personal knowledge and to argument that the certification itself is a separately charged crime. No stipulation concerning the ultimate sufficiency of the evidence is made. The Court will consider the parties' proposed instructions and any Rule 29 motion at the appropriate point in trial.
