UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — SAN FRANCISCO DIVISION
United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
INDICTMENT — COUNT ONE
The grand jury charges that on or about April 7, 2025, in San Francisco, California, in the Northern District of California, Avery R. Denslow knowingly and willfully made a materially false statement in a matter within the jurisdiction of the United States Department of Commerce, an agency of the executive branch of the United States, in violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2). The matter concerned compliance with a federal equipment award made to the fictional Caldera Sound Research Cooperative. The defendant was the cooperative's property administrator. During a recorded compliance interview, when asked about verification of the six instruments identified in the March 13 property certification, the defendant stated: "I checked all six before I signed." The grand jury alleges that the answer represented that the defendant had personally verified the physical presence of all six instruments before signing when, as the defendant then knew, two instruments were not at the facility at the time of the property review and the defendant had not personally examined them before signing.

COUNT ONE — MATERIALITY AND SCOPE
The grand jury further charges that the statement was material to the Department's review of the cooperative's equipment accountability records because the review concerned whether the cooperative had verified the existence and location of award-funded equipment and whether further verification was required before closure of the monitoring item. The allegation concerns the recorded April 7 answer, not a separate offense in making the March 13 certification. The government alleges neither theft of equipment nor a claim for a particular amount of reimbursement in this count. All allegations concerning meaning, falsity, knowledge, willfulness and materiality are matters to be proved at trial. The fact that the Department pursued additional records after the interview does not itself establish or negate the allegations. Venue is alleged because the interview and charged statement occurred in San Francisco. A true bill.
