GOVERNMENT EXHIBIT 1 — AWARD PROPERTY SCHEDULE
Caldera Sound Research Cooperative — fictional award CSRC-24-EQ
Performance start: October 1, 2024. Federal awarding agency: Department of Commerce. This is a simulated award-specific attachment, not a representation of a generally applicable agency regulation.
The property schedule identifies six instruments acquired for the cooperative's coastal measurement program. The six serial identifiers are CS-101, CS-102, CS-103, CS-104, CS-105 and CS-106. Each item remains accountable to the cooperative. The award does not prohibit a temporary offsite transfer for authorized calibration. The cooperative must retain a record sufficient to establish the item's identity, current location, responsible custodian and disposition. The schedule is part of the award file. It does not provide an instruction that all six instruments must remain physically in the same room at all times.

ATTACHMENT B — PERIODIC PROPERTY CHECK
The cooperative shall prepare a periodic property verification record for the award file. A physical check may be combined with reliable third-party custody records for equipment temporarily at a service vendor, provided that the basis of verification is identified accurately. The record should distinguish direct observation from documentary confirmation. The individual signing the verification record is responsible for reviewing the supporting material and identifying exceptions that require follow-up. The signature does not convert a vendor receipt into a personal observation. The agency may request additional records to clarify a location or verification method. A request for clarification is not, by itself, a finding that the item is missing. The agency specialist may close a monitoring item, request additional supporting records or refer an unresolved discrepancy for further review.

ATTACHMENT C — RESPONSIBILITIES
The property coordinator maintains the serial-level register, including transfers for calibration. The program operations director approves temporary service transfers. The property administrator reviews the periodic verification record and certifies the completed review. The federal grant specialist reviews documentation submitted under the award and may seek clarification about the process employed. The review is directed to equipment accountability rather than the scientific performance of the instruments. An instrument identified as at an authorized service vendor may remain accountable if custody and identity are adequately supported. The specialist is not required to visit the cooperative in every monitoring cycle. A discrepancy may be resolved from contemporaneous documents if they answer the question raised. The award file shall retain both the original certification and any later clarification without overwriting either.
