IMPRISONMENT AND SUPERVISED RELEASE
The defendant is committed to the custody of the Bureau of Prisons for a term of six months. Upon release from imprisonment, the defendant shall be on supervised release for one year. The mandatory conditions of supervision apply, together with the standard conditions pronounced in open court and set out in the separate conditions attachment. The Court imposes a special assessment of $100. No restitution is ordered on this record. No fine is imposed. The Court does not adjudicate any civil award dispute or direct the disposition of any agency property. The defendant shall surrender as directed by the United States Marshal, subject to any separate order concerning release pending appeal. The written judgment reflects the sentence announced in open court.

SUPERVISED RELEASE — CONDITIONS ATTACHMENT
During the one-year supervision term the defendant must not commit any federal, state or local offense. The defendant must comply with the mandatory conditions applicable to the offense and the Court's pronouncement, including the prohibition on unlawful possession or use of controlled substances, required testing subject to lawful modification, and lawful reporting requirements. The defendant must report to the probation office in the district to which the defendant is released as directed after release from custody. The defendant must report thereafter as instructed, answer lawful inquiries truthfully, and comply with supervision directions. The defendant must reside at a place approved by the supervising officer and notify the officer of changes as required. Travel outside the authorized district requires permission where applicable. The defendant must permit lawful supervision and refrain from knowingly associating with persons engaged in criminal activity, subject to lawful exceptions and instructions. These are simulated conditions implementing the sentence; no special occupational restriction is imposed.
