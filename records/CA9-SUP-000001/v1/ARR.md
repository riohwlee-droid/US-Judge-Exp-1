UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA — SAN FRANCISCO DIVISION
United States v. Avery R. Denslow — SIM-NDCA-25-CR-014 (fictional)
MINUTE ORDER — INITIAL ARRAIGNMENT
December 8, 2025. The defendant appears with appointed counsel Emmett J. Carrow. The United States appears by Lucia M. Renwick. The defendant confirms receipt of a copy of the indictment and waives formal reading after consultation with counsel. The defendant enters a plea of not guilty to Count One. The Court advises that the charge is an accusation, not evidence. Conditions of release continue under the separate release order; the defendant remains released on personal recognizance subject to ordinary appearance and reporting conditions. The parties report that the recorded interview, property records, and grant correspondence are being provided in discovery. No substantive ruling is made as to admissibility or interpretation. Counsel shall meet and confer concerning production of the recording and jointly identify an accurate transcript for trial use.

SCHEDULING DIRECTIONS
The parties shall propose a discovery and motion calendar following review of the native digital recording and its export. The Court sets a January 12, 2026 status conference and directs counsel to submit any proposed Speedy Trial Act exclusion with a factual showing and a separate proposed order; the Court makes no blanket prospective exclusion by this minute entry. The parties estimate that several custodians and the agency specialist will be required. All requests for continuance must identify the time sought, the reasons for the request, and the position of the opposing party. Trial exhibits should identify the document date, the custodian, the relevant account or file identifier, and whether authenticity is disputed. The defendant's decision whether to testify is reserved. No inference shall be drawn from the defendant's silence. A detailed trial setting order and any excluded-time findings shall be retained separately in the record.
