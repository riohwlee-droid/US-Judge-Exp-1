# COMPLAINT AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL

STATE OF MAINE · SUPERIOR COURT · CUMBERLAND, ss. · CV-2025-418

HARBORLIGHT COLD STORAGE, LLC, Plaintiff v. PENOBSCOT POWER SYSTEMS, INC., Defendant

Plaintiff Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC, through counsel, alleges:

## Parties and transaction

1. Harborlight is a Maine limited liability company operating refrigerated inventory storage near Ellsworth for seafood distributors.

2. Penobscot is a Maine corporation providing temporary generation and emergency electrical services. It undertook the response obligations at issue through a written agreement with Harborlight.

3. This is an action for damages for breach of that agreement. Harborlight alleges a claim against the Provider, not against the electric utility, and does not allege that Provider caused the utility interruption.

4. On May 17, 2024, the parties executed the Emergency Generation Support Agreement, attached as Exhibit A and reproduced at [R-003].

5. The agreement specified an annual standby retainer of $45,600 payable in monthly installments of $3,800. Harborlight paid the installments due through December 2024.

6. The covered undertaking was mobile-generation support for Harborlight's facility. It did not supply a permanent generator on site before the outage.

## Occurrence and performance

7. Utility service to the facility failed at approximately 05:40 on November 14, 2024 during severe coastal weather.

8. After the interruption had continued for more than four hours, Harborlight provided qualifying notice to Defendant at approximately 09:55 that day and requested temporary generation.

9. Under section 3.1, the Provider undertook to furnish suitable temporary generation and complete hookup within twelve hours after a qualifying notice, subject to section 3.2.

10. Section 3.2 excuses delay caused by severe weather, road closure or other conditions beyond the Provider's reasonable control. It also requires commercially reasonable efforts to minimize such delay, including consideration of available alternate equipment or transport routes when practicable.

11. Section 3.3 does not require transportation in violation of a governmental closure order or unreasonable danger to personnel. Harborlight does not allege a duty to violate such an order.

12. Defendant did not provide operating temporary generation at Harborlight until approximately 07:20 on November 16, 2024.

13. Harborlight alleges that reasonable further efforts were practicable after the initial obstruction, including inquiry concerning alternate transport and equipment. Harborlight relies in part on the later route information, the Belfast unit and the timing of Defendant's subsequent response. It does not allege that a nominal generator rating alone establishes the feasibility of a specific shipment.

14. Harborlight maintained an operations contact for site access and performed the payment obligations on which it relies. Defendant has disputed the claim, including breach, causation and loss.

## Count I - breach of contract

15. Plaintiff incorporates paragraphs 1 through 14.

16. The written agreement imposed the obligations described above. Plaintiff alleges that Defendant failed to make commercially reasonable efforts required during the delay and that the failure constituted breach.

17. Plaintiff alleges that the breach caused recoverable inventory loss and response expense. Its initial demand is $103,400, consisting of an asserted $91,400 inventory amount and $12,000 response-expense amount. The amount recoverable, including the appropriate valuation and causal allocation, remains subject to proof.

18. Plaintiff seeks compensation for loss legally attributable to the alleged breach. It does not request compensation merely because utility service failed or merely because an initial weather obstruction occurred.

## Prayer for relief

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff requests judgment for proven compensatory damages; interest to the extent permitted by law; allowable costs; and such further relief as is appropriate on the claim proved. Plaintiff demands a jury trial on issues triable by jury.
