# AFFIDAVIT OF OWEN SUTHERLAND

STATE OF MAINE · SUPERIOR COURT · CUMBERLAND, ss.

Harborlight Cold Storage, LLC v. Penobscot Power Systems, Inc. · CV-2025-418

1. I am president of Penobscot Power Systems, Inc. I supervise dispatch contracts and response planning. I signed the May 17, 2024 agreement for the company. I have reviewed the dispatch extract produced for this motion and identify it as a reproduction from the company's event records.

2. The dispatch record reflects receipt of a qualifying notice from Harborlight at 09:55 on November 14, 2024. I do not dispute that timestamp for purposes of this motion. The utility interruption itself was not a Provider event and was not caused by our equipment.

3. Severe weather affected the initial transport plan. The 10:44 entry records a mobilization pause following a closure report. I did not instruct an employee to drive through a governmental closure. I regarded compliance with road restrictions and the protection of personnel as prerequisites to movement.

4. There are distinctions between equipment readiness and a completed dispatch assignment. A shop-ready entry does not by itself assign a driver, confirm a lawful transport route or establish a compatible connection at the destination. Those matters must be coordinated for an actual mobilization.

5. The Belfast 200-kW unit appearing in the 14:10 entry had a provisional reservation, B-17, for another customer. The extract does not state that the unit had already been delivered to that customer. I do not describe a provisional reservation as a completed delivery. Our position is that a resource may be encumbered for dispatch purposes before it leaves a yard.

6. I believed additional labor and escort resources would be difficult to obtain under the conditions. That belief was part of my operational assessment. I do not identify in this affidavit a written refusal by a named outside carrier on the evening of November 14. No driver's sworn statement accompanies my affidavit.

7. The road bulletin noted in our log at 16:21 describes a conditional commercial detour. I did not personally confirm an alternate route for the particular rig after 16:15 until the following afternoon. I do not offer a road official's certification that every possible route to Harborlight remained closed throughout that period.

8. The fact that I had not personally confirmed a route does not mean I believed one was safe. I considered an unconfirmed route different from a verified dispatch path. The parties disagree about what further investigation was commercially reasonable, and I state here what I personally did and understood rather than assigning legal effect to those facts.

9. The extract records a customer request at 17:08 and a review deferred to the next day. I have reviewed the separate customer email and company response at [R-008]. I do not convert the customer's 17:05 account of a call, the dispatch entry at 17:08 and the email at 17:12 into one exact timestamp. They concern related communications recorded by different participants.

10. Follow-up route checking is recorded at 15:55 on November 15. Dispatch of a truck with compatible equipment is recorded at 16:45. The elapsed interval between dispatch and the 07:20 energization on November 16 is not broken down by this extract into travel, staging and connection tasks. I do not supply a retrospective hour-by-hour account that the submitted log does not contain.

11. The Provider disputes whether Harborlight has established a loss caused by any breach on our part. I am not qualified to determine the precise condition of each seafood lot at a particular hour, and I do not purport to do so. Our objection is to the proof and the allocation of contractual responsibility, not a claim that I personally inspected every discarded item.

12. I understand that the materials attached for this motion are not our entire company archive. The submitted extract records the entries reproduced in [R-007]. I have not attached a complete transport-clearance file, a sworn carrier refusal or an engineer's report. The absence of such materials from my affidavit is not a stipulation to the legal inferences urged by Harborlight.

13. The company does not admit that a 200-kW rating by itself proves the Belfast unit was both available and deployable to Harborlight that evening. Nor do I say that the reservation notation, standing alone, proves the opposite as an engineering matter. Those notations must be read with the scope of the record I have identified.

14. The agreement is authentic. Its interpretation is disputed. I make no representation that the retainer purchased storm insurance, and I do not purport to determine whether its reasonable-efforts language creates an enforceable obligation in the circumstances.
