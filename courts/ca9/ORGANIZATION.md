# CA9 fictional organization and personnel v1

Selected real institutional structure, fictional people. Sources: [court overview](https://www.ca9.uscourts.gov/about), [judges and commission seniority](https://www.ca9.uscourts.gov/about/judges), [28 USC 45](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t28/s45/), [28 USC 711](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t28/s711/), 28 USC 712, 713, 715, 332, 371; [contact and services](https://www.ca9.uscourts.gov/contact/). Court-level administration is distinct from Judicial Council and circuit-wide support. Staff titles and counts, names, dates, schools, chambers locations and career trajectories here are fictional simulation assumptions, not actual personnel or claims of actual staffing authorization. No real private records.

Roster: 36 NPC judges (28 active, 8 senior), player reserved as 29th active seat, 103 planned law clerks and 61 planned administrative/support staff. 37 chambers including player. Active benches: three clerks and one judicial assistant per chamber, senior: two clerks and one assistant. This is a design choice. Shared offices modeled through nine units, including functional overlays rather than asserted official departments. Judicial Council is not a superior merits court.

## Installation
`roster-v1.json` is fixed preparation data, not official active state. Choose player name, fictional birth date, commission date, chambers city and game date. New commission is after NPC commissions, player rank 29. Start must be at or after baseline and chief term must still be valid. Revalidate dates, appointment assumptions, applicable rules and first case; mark profile ready only when genuinely ready. Then campaign.configure and ca9.organization_install in one request; use entire roster-v1 payload and canonical engine.digest(roster), user_confirmed=true. Storage compares to fixed file on replay. No existing CURRENT change during preparation. Clerk term duration is a planning convention; no automatic expiration. No automatic senior transition/chief succession/vacancy fill yet; explicit reviewed future schema needed, historical panels must remain valid.

## NPC protocol
Read only relevant profiles, case records and past actual opinions. Generate individual preliminary reasoning independently before user position is revealed where possible. Save a separate npc_private/internal document and ca9.independent_view event for each NPC on current panel epoch before conference. Do not disclose private initial memo until legally/operationally authorized. Public repo is not a technical privacy boundary. Different interpretation, scope, expertise, tone and revision conditions guide arguments; no numerical ideology-to-vote formula. Change positions only on actual record/authority/reasoning and record changes. Do not invent prior judgments: prior_opinion_ids starts empty; retrieve issued opinions by author ID and current legal status from state, including separate opinions and vacatur. Do not claim fictional cases as real precedent.

## Roles and interactions
A panel judge assigns research, bench memo, draft assistance, record check or administration only to own chambers; shared units require reviewed referral. Law clerk cannot vote, sign a merits opinion or attend confidential conference as of right. Judicial assistant administrative only. Circuit mediators segregate settlement from merits. Clerk handles docket and mandate administratively, subject to authority. En banc coordinator administers call/vote logistics; not a judge. Council/executive cannot dictate merits. ca9.interaction links internal same-case document, actor_id, other_id, category and summary to both participants' logs, without automatic loyalty scores. All actual correspondence is a document, not retroactive characterization. No automatic promotion, firing, external contacts or authority beyond reviewed rules.

## Limits
No independent LLM agents, real personnel, actual staffing budget, comprehensive actual organization chart, automated chief succession/retirement, term-end event, historical fictional precedent corpus, or actual case yet. Future personnel changes must use explicit events; general person.register only accepts parties/counsel/witnesses after installation. For law clerk tasks, supply assigning_judge_id/task_kind and unit_referral_reviewed for shared support.
