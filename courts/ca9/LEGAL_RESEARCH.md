# Ninth Circuit legal research and citation protocol v1

## Boundaries
This is an auditable research workflow, not a comprehensive commercial citator or independent AI researcher. ChatGPT uses the connected CourtListener tools and official sources in the current turn; repository code validates saved evidence offline, not the truth of a search assertion. No API key in public files, no automatic paid API, no scraping background jobs. If a database is unavailable, record the limitation and stop production when material questions are unresolved. Real-law cutoff is campaign legal_cutoff; research can be done later in real time but exclude subsequent cases after cutoff. Original party filings may only cite authorities existing at filing time. Historic legal-rule version, retroactivity, supersession and state-law applicability require review at the relevant date. A case that was good law at a historical filing but later displaced requires a separately reviewed historical use (do not silently change filed text). Fictional precedents remain separate in the simulated precedent ledger, not CourtListener; this version does not yet validate their citations in research packets.

## Research procedure
1. Identify issue, governing law, preservation, standard of review, jurisdiction and possible remedies. Search separately for appellant, appellee, and adverse/limiting authority; save queries, filters, cutoff, findings and limitations for each.
2. CourtListener search by citation and name; analyze_citations on actual draft text, extract_citations for short forms, Id., supra, statutes; read_document by opinion_id, not just cluster. Record case name, reporter citation, court, decision date, publication status, docket where available, panel/en banc, cluster and specific opinion IDs; duplicate clusters may represent same case, reconcile. Do not infer precedential force from number of citing decisions.
3. Obtain actual opinion text and exact reporter page, slip page, paragraph or statutory section. Use official court, US Reports, GovInfo or Congress sources when available; distinguish majority/per curiam, plurality, concurrence, dissent, syllabus and headnotes. Preserve only the necessary actual excerpt with a locator marker on its own line: @@ LOC 893. No invented locator; marker is local metadata, reviewed against original. Hash exact UTF-8 snapshot. Identify proposition, holding/dictum/separate, scope and limitations. Avoid copying third-party editorial material.
4. Treatment: search direct history (rehearing, amended/superseded/vacated, certiorari, appeal/remand); negative treatment (overruled/abrogated/limited, intervening higher authority, statute amendment); later citations (opinions-cited endpoint by cited_opinion, name/citation searches, both duplicate clusters as appropriate). Record actual query, provider, date, cutoff, scope, reviewed count, coverage, and limitations. Read material citing passages, classify per proposition, save exact locator, quote and hashed source. Denial of certiorari is not endorsement. A later panel's criticism is not overruling. Escalate unclear cases; never mark material unresolved hits cleared. Search counts are not proof of completeness.
5. Weight: SCOTUS majority holding on applicable federal law, binding published CA9 panel/en banc holding subject to higher/intervening authority; other circuits/district courts normally persuasive; state highest court on controlling state law only with choice-of-law review. Separate opinions and plurality reasoning not automatically binding; Marks/fragmented holdings require analysis and this conservative verifier cannot certify plurality reasoning as binding. Apply Miller v. Gammie, 335 F.3d 889, 893 (9th Cir. 2003) (en banc), only after explicit comparison of clearly irreconcilable intervening higher authority; not simply because a later panel disagrees. Rule 36-3/FRAP 32.1: CA9 unpublished decisions generally nonprecedential; post-2006 citable, pre-2007 only reviewed exceptions (preclusion/law of case, factual purpose, publication or rehearing conflict). Other courts' unpublished citation rules and required copies reviewed manually. Unpublished nonprecedential weight is not general binding authority.
6. Treatment per proposition: usable_limited (only reviewed scope), limited, overruled, vacated, superseded, abrogated, unresolved. Negative operative treatment prevents general support. A limitation requires explicit explanation for use. Historical or adverse discussion remains allowed. A whole case may contain distinct propositions with differing effect; do not treat a citator color as global truth.
7. Actual document citation audit: every page has actual occurrences (exact string and zero-based character offset in stripped logical page), form, resolved authority/pin and antecedent ID for short cites. Associate each tagged use with a reviewed legal weight, purpose and analysis. Audit all originals, including those with no citations. Connector extraction/verification and human/AI semantic review required; local regex flags common reporter cites and Id./supra only, not every legal citation, neutral cite or paraphrase. Local [[AUTH:ID:PIN]] indexing tag is not conventional Bluebook citation. Keep visible conventional citations in text; metadata can be stripped in a future clean export. Do not cite a quotation as holding without reading surrounding reasoning.
8. Clerk memo: state question, legal cutoff, record facts, controlling/contrary law, treatment, application and alternatives, uncertainties, search log. No preselected outcome. Include document research packet; same for opinion drafts and each revision. Recheck before final issue as necessary at same cutoff, with new document/version if changed. No automatic award of precedential status to concurrence/dissent.

## Integration
Factory blueprint research object: templates/research-fields.json and factory-blueprint.json. All production originals except copied excerpts/indices and hidden evidence receive citation audits; authority reviews and sources must match existing authority metadata. Both sides and adverse search required per issue. No valid release without gates. Existing training fixtures remain training; integration tests use synthetic data, not verified real-case packets.

For later clerk memo/draft: records/.../DOC.md plus law/research/CASE/DOC/packet.json and source snapshots in same directory. packet document_sha256 must equal actual document bytes; compute hash with python or GitHub workflow, not by guessing. document.register research_packet={path:"law/research/CASE/DOC/packet.json",digest:"server_computed"}. Request processor computes canonical packet digest. Storage verifies it, source hashes, cutoff, page audit, scope and uses on replay. Substantive research/bench memo/draft assistance task outputs and opinion.circulate in production require it. Each revision gets new document and packet path. A citation-free administrative or procedural draft can use empty authorities with documented no_authority_reason and a passed audit. No live case exists yet.
