## Verified institutional foundations
- The Supreme Judicial Court is Maine's highest court and, sitting as the Law Court, hears appeals on questions of law from trial courts and specified other bodies. Published Law Court opinions are binding on Maine lower courts.
- Maine statute provides one Chief Justice and six Associate Justices; judicial officers receive seven-year terms, with gubernatorial appointment and Senate confirmation.
- Law clerks serving 2026–27 (August 17, 2026–August 13, 2027) work for the entire Court and are assigned to individual chambers; tasks include bench memoranda, legal research and opinion drafting.
- Appellate review ordinarily uses filed briefs and the record. Oral argument, if held, is not a new evidence hearing. Oral argument is ordinarily at the Cumberland County Courthouse, Portland unless stated otherwise.
- Court rules index includes Maine Rules of Appellate Procedure and Maine Code of Judicial Conduct. Do not assume details, deadlines or internal vote conventions not independently checked.
- Official real-world law and calendar do not certify fictional personnel, calendars, or case assignments.

## Operating cycle, with hard boundaries
1. **Intake:** Clerk's office supplies docketed documents and a published docket status. No assignment without a complete, registered initial record.
2. **Preliminary review:** Bench checks case posture, jurisdiction, standard of review, conflicts and recusal. Law clerk analyses are created only when actually instructed and completed.
3. **Hearings:** Court may consider on briefs or conduct scheduled oral argument. Counsel responds on the submitted record, not through new testimony.
4. **Conference:** Justices exchange arguments and provisional views in a judicially proper setting; clerks are not automatically present. Justice positions may evolve with deliberation.
5. **Opinion work:** Authorized author drafts; colleagues circulate comments, concurrence, dissent, and partial joins. Votes on disposition and joins to specific parts are separately recorded; revision does not carry prior joins.
6. **Publication:** Judicial and clerk accuracy, legal authority and governing procedure must be checked before issuing an opinion; post-issuance consequences are not automatic.
7. **Administration:** Chief Justice may handle branch policy and administration; access and conflicts remain subject to ethical rules.
